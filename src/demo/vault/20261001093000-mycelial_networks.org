#+title: Mycelial networks
#+filetags: :biology:networks:

Mycelium is the branching, thread-like body of a fungus. Individual threads
(/hyphae/) are only a few microns wide, yet a single network can span
hectares and live for centuries.

* Why it matters
Mycelial networks are the original *distributed system*: there is no centre,
every junction can route resources, and damage is healed by growing around it.
That's the metaphor behind [[id:3c9d7e10-2f4b-4a8e-b1c6-7d8e9f0a1b02][Zettelkasten]] style note-taking — value lives in the
connections, not the individual notes.

#+begin_quote
A forest is not a collection of trees; it is a network that happens to have trees in it.
#+end_quote

* Key properties
- Decentralised :: no single point of failure
- Adaptive :: strengthens heavily used paths, prunes idle ones
- Cooperative :: exchanges carbon, water and signals between plants
