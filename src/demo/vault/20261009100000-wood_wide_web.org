#+title: The wood wide web
#+filetags: :biology:forest:networks:

The *wood wide web* is the popular name for common mycorrhizal networks:
fungi that connect the roots of different plants and shuttle carbon,
nitrogen and chemical signals between them.

[[id:7a2b3c40-9d8e-4f1a-b2c3-d4e5f6a7b804][Suzanne Simard]]'s 1997 /Nature/ paper showed carbon moving between birch
and Douglas fir through these links.

* Open questions
- How much transfer is cooperation versus fungal self-interest?
- Do the effects in the lab hold at forest scale?
