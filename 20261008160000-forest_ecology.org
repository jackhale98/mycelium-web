#+title: Forest ecology
#+filetags: :biology:forest:

The study of how trees, fungi, animals and soil interact. Old-growth
forests are dominated by a few large "hub" trees that are densely connected
through [[id:8f3a2c1e-6b4d-4e2a-9c7f-1a2b3c4d5e01][mycelial networks]].

* Succession
Disturbance → pioneer species → mixed forest → old growth. Each stage
changes the soil chemistry for the next.
